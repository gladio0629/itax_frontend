Akkauntni o'chirish

O'zbekchaEnglish

Bu sahifa IZGO va IZGO Driver ilovalaridagi akkauntingizni va unga bog'liq ma'lumotlarni qanday o'chirishni tushuntiradi.

Qanday so'rov yuborish kerak

  1. xogiloy088@gmail.com manziliga xat yuboring.
  2. Mavzu: "IZGO akkauntni o'chirish".
  3. Xatda akkauntingizga bog'langan telefon raqamini va qaysi ilova ekanini (IZGO yoki IZGO Driver) yozing.
  4. Biz shu raqam egasi ekaningizni tasdiqlash uchun SMS kod yoki qo'ng'iroq orqali bog'lanamiz.

So'rov tasdiqlangandan keyin akkaunt 30 kun ichida o'chiriladi.

Nima o'chiriladi

  • ism, familiya va telefon raqami;
  • saqlangan manzillar va joylashuv tarixi;
  • saqlangan to'lov usullari (provayder tokenlari);
  • haydovchilar uchun: hujjatlar va selfi suratlari.

Nima saqlanib qoladi

Qonun talab qiladigan hisob-kitob va to'lov yozuvlari (safarlar summasi, sanasi, hamyon harakatlari) qonunda belgilangan muddat davomida saqlanadi, keyin o'chiriladi. Ular boshqa maqsadda ishlatilmaydi.

Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your account in the IZGO and IZGO Driver apps and the data linked to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email xogiloy088@gmail.com.
  2. Subject: "IZGO account deletion".
  3. Include the phone number linked to your account and which app it is (IZGO or IZGO Driver).
  4. We will contact you by SMS code or phone call to confirm you own the number.

Once confirmed, the account is deleted within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • name and phone number;
  • saved addresses and location history;
  • saved payment methods (provider tokens);
  • for drivers: documents and selfie photos.

What is kept

Accounting and payment records required by law (trip amounts, dates, wallet transactions) are kept for the legally required period and then deleted. They are not used for any other purpose.
