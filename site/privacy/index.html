Ushbu siyosat IZGO (yo'lovchilar uchun "IZGO" va haydovchilar uchun "IZGO Driver" mobil ilovalari, birgalikda — "Xizmat") qanday ma'lumotlarni yig'ishi, ulardan qanday foydalanishi va kimga berishini tushuntiradi. Xizmatni O'g'iloy Xasanova (O'zbekiston) taqdim etadi.

1. Qanday ma'lumotlarni yig'amiz

  • Akkaunt: telefon raqami (SMS kod bilan kirish uchun), ism va familiya.
  • Joylashuv: ilova ochiq paytda olish va tushirish nuqtasini aniqlash uchun. Haydovchilar ilovasida — safar paytida mashina joylashuvi yo'lovchiga ko'rsatilishi uchun. Joylashuv faqat siz ruxsat berganingizda olinadi.
  • Safarlar: qayerdan va qayerga, sana va vaqt, o'rindiq yoki yuk turi, narx, baho va izohlar.
  • To'lov: to'lov usuli (naqd, karta, Payme, Click). Karta raqami bizning serverlarimizga tushmaydi — uni to'lov provayderi qayta ishlaydi; biz faqat provayder bergan tokenni va kartaning oxirgi 4 raqamini saqlaymiz.
  • Haydovchilar: ro'yxatdan o'tish uchun hujjatlar, selfi, mashina ma'lumotlari va hamyon harakatlari tarixi.
  • Murojaatlar: yordam so'rovlaringiz matni va aloqa ma'lumotlari.
  • Texnik ma'lumot: qurilma turi, ilova versiyasi, xatoliklar haqidagi yozuvlar va bildirishnoma tokeni.

2. Ma'lumotlardan nima uchun foydalanamiz

  • Akkauntga kirish va uni himoya qilish;
  • buyurtmani haydovchiga yetkazish, narxni hisoblash va safarni bajarish;
  • to'lovlarni amalga oshirish va hisob-kitob yuritish;
  • firibgarlikning oldini olish va xavfsizlik;
  • murojaatlaringizga javob berish va Xizmatni yaxshilash;
  • qonun talablarini bajarish.

Biz ma'lumotlaringizni sotmaymiz va reklama maqsadida boshqalarga bermaymiz.

3. Kimga beramiz

  • Safar ishtirokchilari: buyurtma qabul qilinganda yo'lovchiga haydovchining ismi, surati va mashinasi, haydovchiga esa yo'lovchining ismi va olish nuqtasi ko'rsatiladi.
  • Xizmat ko'rsatuvchilar: to'lov provayderlari (Payme, Click, bank), SMS provayderi, xarita xizmati (Mapbox), server va bildirishnoma xizmatlari (Google Firebase, Apple Push) — faqat o'z vazifasini bajarishi uchun zarur hajmda.
  • Davlat organlari: qonunda belgilangan hollarda va haydovchini qonuniy tekshirish uchun.

4. Saqlash muddati

Ma'lumotlar akkauntingiz faol bo'lgan davrda va qonunda talab qilingan muddat davomida saqlanadi. Keyin ular o'chiriladi yoki shaxsni aniqlab bo'lmaydigan holga keltiriladi.

5. Xavfsizlik

Ma'lumotlar shifrlangan aloqa (HTTPS) orqali uzatiladi, serverlarga kirish cheklangan. Hech bir tizim 100% xavfsiz emas, lekin biz ma'lumotlaringizni himoya qilish uchun oqilona choralarni ko'ramiz.

6. Sizning huquqlaringiz

7. Bolalar

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what data IZGO (the "IZGO" passenger app and the "IZGO Driver" app, together the "Service") collects, how it is used and with whom it is shared. The Service is provided by O'g'iloy Xasanova (Uzbekistan).

1. Data we collect

  • Account: phone number (for SMS sign-in), first and last name.
  • Location: while the app is open, to set pickup and drop-off points. In the driver app, the car's location during a trip so the passenger can see it. Collected only with your permission.
  • Trips: origin and destination, date and time, seats or cargo type, price, ratings and comments.
  • Payments: payment method (cash, card, Payme, Click). Card numbers never reach our servers; they are processed by the payment provider. We store only the provider's token and the last 4 digits.
  • Drivers: documents, selfie and vehicle details for registration, and wallet transaction history.
  • Support: the content of your support requests and contact details.
  • Technical data: device type, app version, error logs and push notification token.

2. How we use data

  • to sign you in and protect your account;
  • to deliver orders to drivers, calculate prices and complete trips;
  • to process payments and keep accounting records;
  • to prevent fraud and keep the Service safe;
  • to answer your requests and improve the Service;
  • to comply with the law.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • Trip participants: once an order is accepted, the passenger sees the driver's name, photo and car; the driver sees the passenger's name and pickup point.
  • Service providers: payment providers (Payme, Click, bank), SMS provider, map service (Mapbox), hosting and push notification services (Google Firebase, Apple Push), only as needed for their task.
  • Authorities: where required by law and for legally required driver verification.

4. Retention

Data is kept while your account is active and for as long as the law requires, then deleted or anonymised.

5. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and server access is restricted. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access or correct your data, or to delete your account, through the in-app "Help" section or at xogiloy088@gmail.com. You can turn off location access in your phone settings at any time.

7. Children

The Service is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
